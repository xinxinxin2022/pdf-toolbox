Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PDFToolBox. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us.

General Questions

Is PDFToolBox completely free?

Yes! All PDFToolBox tools are 100% free to use. There are no hidden charges, no premium tiers, and no feature locks. We keep the site running through unobtrusive advertising.

Do I need to create an account?

No account needed. You can start using any tool immediately — no email, no registration, no hassle.

Are my files safe?

Absolutely. All PDF processing happens directly in your browser. Your files never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server. We literally cannot see your documents.

What browsers are supported?

PDFToolBox works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We recommend keeping your browser updated for the best experience.

Can I use PDFToolBox offline?

Once loaded, most tools can work without an internet connection since processing happens locally. However, you need internet to initially load the site.

Files & Limits

Is there a file size limit?

There is no hard limit, but very large files (over 100MB) may be slow depending on your device's capabilities. For best results, we recommend files under 50MB.

Are there limits on how many files I can process?

There are no daily limits or processing caps. Use our tools as much as you need.

Tools & Features

What is the difference between merge and split?

Merge combines multiple PDF files into a single document. Split does the opposite — it takes one PDF and extracts pages into separate files, or divides the document into sections.

Does compressing a PDF reduce quality?

Our compression tool optimizes the PDF internally to reduce file size while maintaining acceptable quality. Most users won't notice a visual difference.

Can I convert scanned PDFs to Word?

Our PDF to Word tool extracts text from text-based PDFs. Scanned documents require OCR technology, which is not currently supported. We plan to add OCR in the future.

How does PDF to JPG conversion work?

Each page of your PDF is rendered in your browser using PDF.js, then exported as a high-quality JPG image. You can download pages individually or as a ZIP archive.

Can I add a watermark to specific pages only?

Yes! Our watermark tool lets you choose which pages receive the watermark. You can apply it to all pages or select specific ones.

What does the unlock tool do?

The unlock tool removes owner-level restrictions from PDFs (printing, editing, copying limits). It cannot remove user-level encryption passwords that require a password to open the file.

Privacy & Security

Do you store my files after processing?

No. We never store, access, or transmit your documents. Everything happens in your browser. Once you close the tab or refresh, all file data is gone.

Is unlocking a PDF legal?

Yes, removing restrictions from PDFs you own is legal. Just make sure you have the right to modify the document.

Technical

Can I use PDFToolBox on mobile?

Yes! PDFToolBox is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. The interface adapts to your screen size for easy use on any device.

Why is my conversion not perfect?

Since all processing runs client-side in your browser, some complex conversions may have limitations. For best results, use text-based PDFs rather than scanned documents.

How is PDFToolBox different from other PDF tools?

Three key differences: (1) 100% privacy — files never leave your browser; (2) Completely free — no premium tier, no watermarks; (3) No account required — start using tools instantly.
