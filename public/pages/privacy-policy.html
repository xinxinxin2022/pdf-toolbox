Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2024

At PDFToolBox, we take your privacy extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our website.

1. No File Uploads

This is the most important thing you need to know: PDFToolBox processes all PDF files entirely in your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server. When you use our tools to merge, split, compress, or convert PDF files, all processing happens locally on your device. We have zero access to your documents.

2. Data We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to operate the website:

  • Cookies: We use essential cookies for language preference and theme settings. These are stored locally in your browser.
  • Analytics: We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This data is anonymous and does not include any personal information or file content.
  • Advertising: We use Google AdSense, which may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can learn about Google's advertising policies at google.com/policies/technologies/ads/.

3. What We Don't Collect

  • We do NOT collect your PDF files
  • We do NOT collect personal information (name, email, etc.) unless you contact us
  • We do NOT track which tools you use with which files
  • We do NOT sell any data to third parties
  • We do NOT require registration or accounts

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdSense: May use cookies for ad targeting. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • GitHub Pages: Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect access logs. See GitHub's privacy policy.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any data we may hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Disable cookies in your browser settings

6. Children's Privacy

PDFToolBox is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ben357753@163.com.

9. Summary

PDFToolBox is designed with privacy at its core. Your files never leave your browser. We collect minimal data. We don't require accounts. We believe privacy is a fundamental right, and our tools are built to respect that.
