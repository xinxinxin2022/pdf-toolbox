Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I reduce the file size?

Compression results vary depending on the PDF content. PDFs with many images typically see more reduction. Results typically range from 10-50% size reduction.

Will compression reduce quality?

Our compression focuses on internal optimization with minimal quality impact. The output should look nearly identical to the original.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no hard limit, but very large files (over 100MB) may be slow to process in the browser.
