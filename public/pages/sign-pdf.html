Frequently Asked Questions

Is an electronic signature legally binding?

In most countries, electronic signatures are legally binding for most document types. Check your local regulations for specific requirements.

Is my signature uploaded to a server?

No. Your signature is drawn and applied entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Can I sign multiple pages?

Yes, your signature will be placed on every page of the PDF automatically.
