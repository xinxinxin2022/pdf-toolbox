About This Tool

Edit your PDF directly in the browser. Add text annotations, freehand drawings, and images to your PDF pages. All processing happens locally — your documents never leave your device. Perfect for annotating documents, marking up forms, or adding visual notes without any server uploads.

How to Use

  1. Upload your PDF file
  2. Use toolbar to add text, drawings, or images
  3. Navigate pages and place annotations
  4. Click "Export PDF" to download your edited file

Features

  • Add text annotations

  • Insert images (PNG/JPG)
  • Multi-page support
  • Undo/redo history
  • 100% client-side processing

Why Use Edit PDF?

PDFToolBox Editor lets you annotate PDFs directly in your browser. Add text, drawings, and images without uploading to any server. Perfect for marking up documents, adding notes, or making visual edits. All processing stays on your device for complete privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I add to my PDF?

You can add text annotations, freehand drawings, and images (PNG/JPG) to any page of your PDF.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. All editing happens directly in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Can I edit existing text in the PDF?

This tool adds new content (text, drawings, images) on top of your PDF. It does not modify existing text — that requires more advanced PDF editing software.

Can I undo my changes?

Yes! Use the Undo/Redo buttons in the toolbar to step through your edit history.
