Frequently Asked Questions

What Word formats are supported?

We support DOCX files (Word 2007 and later). Older DOC format files should be saved as DOCX first.

Will formatting be preserved?

Basic formatting including text styles, fonts, and layout is preserved. Very complex formatting may have minor differences.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no hard limit, but very large documents may be slow to process in the browser.
